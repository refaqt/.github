## In plain words (this box is not part of the addendum)

Use this addendum when people contribute for a company, a university, or another organisation
that may own rights in their work, for example employees, or a supplier's or partner's staff.
The company signs it once and names the people who may contribute for it. The rules are the
same as in the [Refaqt Contributor Licence Agreement](CLA.md). Each person also accepts that
agreement with the usual comment on their first pull request.

Belgian law, company number BE 0804.145.539, with its registered office at

1. **Same terms.** The Company accepts the Refaqt Contributor Licence Agreement version 1.0 (the

   that controls it, or that is under the same control ("**Affiliates**"). Control means owning
   more than 50% of the shares or votes, or the power to appoint the majority of the board.

2. **Authorised Contributors.** The people in Schedule A ("**Authorised Contributors**") may
   Submit Contributions for the Company. Every Contribution that an Authorised Contributor Submits
   to a Repository is a Contribution of the Company under the Agreement, and the Company gives the
   licences in section 2 of the Agreement for it, also for rights that the Company or its
   Affiliates own.

3. **Changes to the list.** The Company can add or remove people by email to the address in
   section 9 of the Agreement, sent by a person who may represent the Company. A removal applies
   only to Contributions submitted after we receive the email.

4. **The Company's promises.** The Company promises that:
   - it has the right to give the licences in the Agreement for the work of its Authorised
     Contributors;
   - each Authorised Contributor knows the confidentiality rules in section 4 of the Agreement and
     must follow them;
   - the person who signs below may represent the Company.

5. **The person's own acceptance.** Each Authorised Contributor also accepts the Agreement
   personally, with the comment in section 7.1 of the Agreement. That covers any rights that the
   person owns themselves.

6. **Other contracts.** A service, supply, or partnership contract between the Company and
   Refaqt that gives Refaqt more rights than the Agreement applies on those points.
