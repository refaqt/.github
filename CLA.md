## In plain words (this box is not part of the agreement)

- You keep the copyright and the other rights in your work.
- You give Refaqt a permanent, free, worldwide licence to use your work in every way, and to
  license it to others under any licence. That includes open licences such as CERN-OHL-S, GPL
  and CC BY-SA, and paid licences such as the Refaqt OEM licence.
- If you contribute to a public repository, Refaqt promises that your work also stays available
  under the open licence that the repository used on the day you contributed.
- You promise that the work is yours to give, and you tell us about any parts that come from
  someone else.
- Material in private repositories is confidential.
- One acceptance covers all your contributions to all Refaqt repositories.
- If you contribute for a company, the company signs the
  [company addendum](CLA-entity.md) as well.

1.1 **Contribution** means any work that you Submit to us, and any part of it. This includes,

earlier and later versions.

1.3 **Repository** means any repository in the GitHub organisation `refaqt`
(https://github.com/refaqt), public or private, and any other place where we keep Material and
where we refer to this agreement.

1.4 **Public Repository** means a Repository that anyone can read. **Private Repository** means
a Repository that only invited people can read.

1.5 **Submit** means to send to us or to a Repository in any form: for example a pull request, a
commit, an issue, a comment, an email, or a file upload, when it is meant to become part of the
Material. Material that you mark clearly as "not a contribution" is not Submitted.

1.6 **Submission Date** means the date on which you Submit a Contribution.

including the database right of the maker (sui generis right); rights in designs, registered

world, including applications for them. It does **not** include trademarks, trade names, or
logos.

1.8 **Patent Claims** means every claim of a patent or patent application, anywhere in the
world, that you own or control now or later, and that would be infringed by the Contribution
alone or by the Contribution combined with the Material.

1.9 **Product** means any physical object made fully or partly from the Contribution or from
Material that contains the Contribution.

### 3. Your promises

3.1 **Your own work.** The Contribution is your own original work, or you have the right to
Submit it under this agreement.

3.2 **Employer and clients.** If an employer, a client, a university, or someone else may have
rights in your Contribution (for example because you made it at work or under a contract), you
promise that they have given you written permission to Submit it under this agreement, or that
they have signed the [company addendum](CLA-entity.md).

3.3 **Material from others.** If any part of a Contribution comes from someone else (for example
supplier CAD models, datasheets, standard part libraries, or code from another project), you
will say so clearly when you Submit it. You will name the source and the licence, and you will
keep its notices. You will only Submit it if its licence allows us to use it in the Material.
The licences in section 2 do not cover that part, and that part stays under its own licence.

3.4 **No secrets of others.** The Contribution does not contain confidential information or trade
secrets that belong to someone else.

3.5 **No known claims.** As far as you know, the Contribution does not infringe the rights of
anyone else.

3.6 **Legal capacity.** You are of legal age and able to accept this agreement. If you are not,
your parent or guardian accepts it for you.

3.7 **Changes.** If you learn that one of these promises is no longer true, you will tell us
quickly at the address in section 9.

### 4. Confidentiality in Private Repositories

4.1 Everything that you see or receive in a Private Repository is confidential, unless we have
published it or it was already public without a breach of this agreement.

4.2 You will use it only to work on Refaqt projects. You will not share it with anyone else
without our written permission. You will protect it with at least the same care that you use for
your own confidential information.

4.3 This duty lasts during your work on the Repository and for five years after your access
ends. It lasts longer for trade secrets, for as long as they stay secret.

4.4 A separate secrecy agreement (NDA) between you and us takes priority over this section where
the two differ.

5.1 Except for the promises in sections 3 and 4, you give the Contribution "as is". You give no
guarantee that it works or is fit for a purpose, and you have no duty to support it.

5.2 Neither party is liable to the other for indirect damage, such as lost profit or lost data,
caused by this agreement, as far as the law allows.

5.3 Nothing in this agreement limits liability for fraud, for intentional fault, or for anything
else for which the law does not allow a limit.

### 6. What we do with your Contribution

6.1 We do not have to use, publish, or keep your Contribution.

6.2 We record who made each Contribution in the history of the Repository. That record is the
normal way in which we name contributors.

6.3 We may transfer this agreement, and the licences in it, to a company or organisation that
takes over the Material or the Repositories. If we do, the promises in section 2.4 bind that
company or organisation too.

### 7. How you accept this agreement

7.1 You accept this agreement by one of these:

- (a) posting this exact sentence as a comment on a pull request in a Repository:
  "**I have read the Refaqt Contributor Licence Agreement version 1.0 and I agree to it.**"; or
- (b) signing it on paper or with an electronic signature.

7.2 From then on, the agreement applies to the Contribution in that pull request, and to each
later Contribution at the moment that you Submit it. Each time that you Submit a Contribution,
you confirm again that you accept this agreement for that Contribution.

7.3 You can stop accepting this agreement for future Contributions by telling us in writing at
the address in section 9. That does not change the licences for Contributions that you already
Submitted.

7.4 We may publish new versions of this agreement. A new version applies to you only if you
accept it. Until then, this version keeps applying to your Contributions.

8.6 You have the right to see, correct, and in some cases delete or limit this data, and to
object. Write to the address in section 9. You may also complain to the Belgian Data Protection
